      <div style="flex: 2; font-size: 18px; text-align: justify;">
        <p>Alcohol-specific deaths refer to fatalities caused solely by alcohol consumption, where alcohol is the direct and underlying cause of death. These deaths are attributed to conditions such as <b>liver cirrhosis, alcohol poisoning, and alcoholic hepatitis</b>, which result from prolonged alcohol misuse or acute alcohol intoxication. In the United Kingdom, alcohol-specific deaths have been rising, making them a critical public health issue. These deaths vary significantly across different demographics (age and sex), geographical regions, and time periods. Identifying these variations can reveal high-risk groups and trends, providing valuable insights for healthcare professionals, policymakers, and public health organizations.</p>

    <li><b>Policy and Healthcare Interventions:</b> Policymakers need accurate data to implement effective alcohol control policies.</li>

    <h2 style="color: #2c3e50;">What This Project Shows</h2><img src="output/combined.png" width="800" height="600" style="display: block; margin: auto;" alt="combined">
    <p>Our visualizations provide key insights into alcohol-specific deaths:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Who is most affected:</strong> Middle-aged males (40–69 years) are disproportionately impacted, making them the most vulnerable demographic.</li>
      <li><strong>Where deaths are highest:</strong> Scotland and northern England have the most alcohol-specific deaths, highlighting regional disparities.</li>
      <li><strong>How trends have changed:</strong> Deaths have steadily increased over the years, especially from 2015 onward, pointing to an escalating public health crisis.</li>
      <li><strong>Differences between regions and sexes:</strong> Males dominate in all regions, but Wales and Northern Ireland show significantly fewer deaths compared to Scotland and England.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>These insights offer a comprehensive view of the issue, helping decision-makers prioritize interventions and allocate resources effectively.</p>
    <hr style="border: 1px solid #ccc;">
    <h2 style="color: #2c3e50;">Why This Matters</h2>
    <p>Alcohol-specific deaths are preventable, and understanding the factors contributing to these fatalities is essential for saving lives. By highlighting trends and disparities, this project supports:</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Targeted Health Campaigns:</strong> Public health initiatives can be tailored to at-risk groups, such as middle-aged males in high-mortality regions.</li>
      <li><strong>Resource Allocation:</strong> Policymakers can direct funding and resources to regions and populations most affected by alcohol-related harm.</li>
      <li><strong>Informed Policy Development:</strong> Insights from the data can shape policies like minimum alcohol pricing and expanded addiction recovery programs.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Every life saved represents a win for individuals, families, and communities, reducing the broader societal burden of alcohol-related harm.</p>
